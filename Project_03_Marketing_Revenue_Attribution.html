Marketing-to-Revenue Attribution

Problem

High lead volume but low revenue growth. Trace the journey from campaign exposure → lead → conversion → revenue and show where the funnel loses value.

Algorithm flow

InputProcessDecisionOutput
Ingest ad touchpoints, CRM leads, opportunities, closed-won revenue
Stitch identities (cookie / email / CRM ID) into customer journeys
Build funnel stages: Impression → Lead → MQL → Opportunity → Customer
Compute stage conversion & drop-off per channel / campaign
Attribution model? First-touch / Last-touch / Linear / Time-decay
Distribute revenue credit across touchpoints by chosen model
Value-leak = leads × (benchmark conv − actual conv) × avg deal value
Funnel leak map + credited revenue per channel

Pseudocode

journeys = stitch(touchpoints, crm)
for j in journeys:
    credit = MODEL[model](j.touches)          # weights sum to 1
    for t, w in zip(j.touches, credit):
        revenue_by_channel[t.channel] += w * j.revenue
for ch in channels:
    for stage in STAGES:
        ch.conv[stage] = count(stage+1) / count(stage)
        ch.leak[stage] = count(stage) * (BENCH[stage] - ch.conv[stage]) * AVG_DEAL
worst = argmax(leak)   # where the funnel loses most value
